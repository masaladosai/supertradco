## SRVCABLE Research Brief

### Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables Ltd (NSE: SRVCABLE) manufactures power cables, wires, and conductors [S1]. For Q1 FY27 (quarter ended June 30, 2026), the company reported revenue from operations of ₹1,248 crore (+18.0% YoY), Profit After Tax of ₹82 crore (+5.9% YoY), and EBITDA of ₹140 crore (+2.6% YoY) [S1]. The EBITDA margin for the quarter was 11.2%, a decline of 170 basis points year-on-year [S1].

### Bull case
*   The company's order book increased to ₹3,900 crore as of June 30, 2026, up from ₹3,150 crore a year prior [S1].
*   Exports contributed 21% of revenue in Q1 FY27, an increase from 16% in Q1 FY26 [S1].
*   A new plant in Bharuch, Gujarat, which is expected to add approximately 30% to cable capacity, is on track to begin commercial production in Q3 FY27 and will contribute from the second half of FY27 [S1, S2].
*   Management maintains a revenue growth guidance of 15-17% for the full year FY27 [S2].
*   Demand from power transmission and data-centre projects remains strong [S1]. Data-centre cables currently represent about 9% of domestic revenue and are projected to double in two years [S2].
*   Management expects to pass on higher copper prices to customers over the next one to two quarters, as approximately two-thirds of contracts include price-variation clauses [S1, S2].
*   The company's net debt to equity ratio is low at 0.35 times [S2].
*   Promoter pledged shares have significantly decreased to 4.1% of promoter holding as of June 2026, down from 11.5% in June 2025 [S4]. Foreign and Domestic Institutional Investor holdings have also increased [S4].

### Bear case
*   EBITDA margins contracted by 170 basis points in Q1 FY27, primarily due to a sharp rise in copper prices [S1, S5].
*   Receivable days increased from 78 to 96 over the last year, mainly due to slower payments from two large state utility customers [S2].
*   The company has received a GST demand order for ₹46.3 crore (including interest and penalty) related to input tax credit claims for FY2020-21 and FY2021-22 [S3].
*   A news report from March 2024 highlighted concerns about promoter pledging, which had risen to 35% of promoter holding at that time [S6].

### Open questions
*   There is a material contradiction in reported Q1 FY27 revenue: the company's official press release states ₹1,248 crore (+18.0% YoY) [S1], while a news article reports ₹1,428 crore (+35% YoY) [S5].
*   While management expects to pass on higher copper costs, an analyst notes that investors will be watching to see if the company can successfully implement this [S1, S5].
*   The company believes it has a strong case against the ₹46.3 crore GST demand and does not expect a material impact, but the outcome of the appeal is uncertain [S3].
*   It is unclear if "Sarvottam Cable Network," a local cable TV and broadband operator penalized ₹8 lakh for illegal overhead wiring in Nagpur [S8], is related to Sarvottam Cables Ltd (NSE: SRVCABLE), a manufacturer of power cables, wires, and conductors [S1]. The business descriptions differ.
