**Sarvottam Cables Ltd (NSE: SRVCABLE)**

**Snapshot**

Sarvottam Cables Ltd is a manufacturer of power cables, wires, and conductors [S1]. The company reported an 18.0% year-over-year (YoY) revenue growth in Q1 FY27, driven by strong demand from power transmission and data-centre projects [S1].

*   **Q1 FY27 Revenue from Operations:** ₹1,248 crore (+18.0% YoY) [S1].
    *   *Note on conflicting data: A news article reported Q1 FY27 revenue as ₹1,428 crore (+35% YoY) [S5]. However, we prioritize the company's official press release which states ₹1,248 crore [S1].*
*   **Q1 FY27 Profit After Tax (PAT):** ₹82 crore (+5.9% YoY) [S1].
*   **Q1 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 11.2% (−170 bps YoY) [S1].
*   **Order Book (as of 30 June 2026):** ₹3,900 crore, up from ₹3,150 crore a year ago [S1].
*   **Exports Contribution:** 21% of revenue in Q1 FY27 (vs. 16% in Q1 FY26) [S1].
*   **New Bharuch Plant:** On track to begin commercial production in Q3 FY27, adding approximately 30% to cable capacity [S1].
*   **Net Debt to Equity:** 0.35x [S2].
*   **Promoter Holding (as of 30 June 2026):** 58.2%, with 4.1% of promoter holding pledged [S4].

**Bull Case**

*   **Robust Demand & Order Book:** Sarvottam Cables reported strong Q1 FY27 revenue growth and maintains a 15-17% revenue growth guidance for the full FY27 [S1, S2]. The significant increase in its order book to ₹3,900 crore provides strong revenue visibility for the coming quarters [S1]. Demand from power transmission and data-centre projects is particularly strong [S1].
*   **Capacity Expansion & High-Growth Segments:** The new Bharuch plant, expected to commence commercial production in Q3 FY27, will boost cable manufacturing capacity by about 30% and contribute to revenue from the second half of FY27 [S1, S2]. The company is strategically focused on high-growth areas like data-centre cables, which currently constitute about 9% of domestic revenue and are projected to double in two years [S2]. Export revenue contribution also saw a healthy increase [S1].
*   **Expected Margin Recovery & Strong Financials:** Despite Q1 FY27 margin contraction due to rising copper prices, management expects to pass on these costs over the next one to two quarters, as approximately two-thirds of contracts include price-variation clauses [S1, S2]. The company maintains a healthy balance sheet with a low net debt-to-equity ratio of 0.35x, with the Bharuch capex largely funded by internal accruals [S2]. Promoter pledging has also significantly reduced to 4.1% of promoter holding as of June 2026 [S4].

**Bear Case**

*   **Persistent Margin Pressure:** While management expects to pass on increased copper costs, the lag of one to two quarters could mean continued pressure on EBITDA margins in the near term [S1, S2]. The ability to fully and timely pass on these costs is crucial for profitability recovery.
*   **Working Capital Strain from Receivables:** Receivable days increased from 78 to 96 over the last year, primarily due to slower payments from two large state utility customers [S2]. Although the company is monitoring this and has not reported defaults, a sustained increase in receivables could strain working capital and cash flows.
*   **Regulatory & Legal Uncertainty:** The company has received a GST demand order of ₹46.3 crore (including interest and penalty) for FY2020-21 and FY2021-22 [S3]. While Sarvottam Cables intends to appeal and expects no material impact, the outcome of the appeal process and any potential financial implications remain an overhang [S3].

**Open Questions**

*   **Pace of Margin Recovery:** How quickly and effectively can Sarvottam Cables pass on the increased copper costs to customers, and what is the expected timeline for EBITDA margin normalization? [S1, S2]
*   **Receivable Management Strategy:** What specific measures will the company implement to reduce its receivable days, particularly with state utility customers, and mitigate potential working capital risks? [S2]
*   **Bharuch Plant Ramp-up & Profitability:** What is the expected utilization rate and profitability contribution from the new Bharuch plant in its initial quarters of commercial production (Q3 FY27 onwards)? [S1, S2]
*   **Resolution of GST Demand:** What is the anticipated timeline for the appeal process regarding the ₹46.3 crore GST demand, and what are the potential financial and operational risks if the appeal is unsuccessful? [S3]
*   **Data-Centre Segment Execution:** What are the detailed plans and investments required to achieve the target of doubling data-centre cables' contribution to domestic revenue in two years, and what are the competitive dynamics in this segment? [S2]
