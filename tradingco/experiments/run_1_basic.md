## SRVCABLE: Research Brief

**Ticker:** SRVCABLE
**Company:** Sarvottam Cables Ltd (NSE: SRVCABLE) is a manufacturer of power cables, wires, and conductors [S1].

---

### Snapshot

*   **Q1 FY27 Performance:** Sarvottam Cables reported revenue from operations of ₹1,248 crore, an 18.0% increase year-over-year (YoY) [S1]. Profit after tax (PAT) grew 5.9% YoY to ₹82 crore [S1]. EBITDA margin contracted by 170 basis points (bps) to 11.2% [S1].
*   **Order Book:** The company's order book stood at ₹3,900 crore as of June 30, 2026, up from ₹3,150 crore a year ago (+23.8% YoY) [S1].
*   **Capacity Expansion:** A new plant in Bharuch, Gujarat, which will add approximately 30% to cable capacity, is on track to begin commercial production in Q3 FY27 [S1].
*   **Exports & Data Centres:** Exports contributed 21% of revenue in Q1 FY27, up from 16% in Q1 FY26 [S1]. Data-centre cables currently represent 9% of domestic revenue and are expected to double in two years [S2].
*   **Financial Position:** Net debt to equity is 0.35 times [S2].
*   **Shareholding:** As of June 30, 2026, Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) and Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) increased their stake to 11.8% and 14.6% respectively. The percentage of pledged promoter shares decreased to 4.1% of promoter holding [S4].

---

### Bull Case

*   **Strong Demand & Order Book:** The company benefits from robust demand in power transmission and data-centre projects, evidenced by a 23.8% YoY growth in its order book to ₹3,900 crore [S1]. Management maintains its FY27 revenue growth guidance of 15-17% [S2].
*   **Capacity-led Growth:** The upcoming Bharuch plant, adding 30% to cable capacity and commencing production in Q3 FY27, is expected to fuel future revenue growth and meet increasing demand [S1, S2].
*   **High-Growth Segments:** The data-centre cable segment, currently 9% of domestic revenue, is projected to double in two years, indicating a strong growth avenue in a specialized, potentially higher-margin area [S2].
*   **Expected Margin Recovery:** Despite Q1 FY27 margin contraction due to higher copper prices, management expects to pass on these costs over the next two quarters, as approximately two-thirds of contracts include price-variation clauses [S1, S2].
*   **Positive Shareholding Trends:** Increasing stakes by FIIs and DIIs, coupled with a significant reduction in promoter share pledging (to 4.1% of promoter holding), suggests growing institutional confidence and improved governance perception [S4].

---

### Bear Case

*   **Persistent Margin Pressure:** Q1 FY27 EBITDA margins contracted by 170 bps, primarily due to a sharp rise in copper prices [S1, S5]. While cost pass-through is expected, the 1-2 quarter lag could continue to impact profitability in the near term, and future commodity price volatility remains a risk [S2].
*   **Increasing Receivables:** Receivable days increased from 78 to 96 over the last year, mainly due to slower payments from two large state utility customers [S2]. This could strain working capital and cash flows if not effectively managed.
*   **Regulatory Challenge:** The company has received a GST demand order of ₹46.3 crore (including interest and penalty) for FY2020-21 and FY2021-22 [S3]. Although the company intends to appeal and expects no material impact, it introduces a legal and financial uncertainty.
*   **Revenue Discrepancy:** While the official company release (S1) reported Q1 FY27 revenue as ₹1,248 crore (+18.0% YoY), a news article (S5) cited ₹1,428 crore (+35% YoY). This discrepancy, though likely an error in the news report, could lead to investor confusion.

---

### Open Questions

*   **Margin Recovery Timeline & Extent:** How quickly and completely can Sarvottam Cables pass on the increased copper costs to customers, and what is the expected timeline for EBITDA margins to return to previous levels or improve further? [S1, S2]
*   **Receivables Management Strategy:** What specific actions are being taken to reduce the elevated receivable days, particularly with state utility customers, and what is the potential impact on cash flow if these efforts are not successful? [S2]
*   **GST Demand Resolution:** What is the anticipated timeline for the appeal process regarding the ₹46.3 crore GST demand, and what is the potential financial exposure or impact on the company's reputation if the appeal is unsuccessful? [S3]
*   **Bharuch Plant Ramp-up:** What are the specific revenue and profitability targets for the new Bharuch plant in its initial quarters of commercial production, and what is the expected utilization rate and contribution to overall capacity? [S1, S2]
*   **Data-centre Cable Market:** What is the competitive landscape for data-centre cables, and what specific strategies are in place to achieve the targeted doubling of revenue from this segment within two years? [S2]
