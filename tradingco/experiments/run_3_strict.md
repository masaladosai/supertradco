## SRVCABLE Research Brief

### Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables Ltd (NSE: SRVCABLE) manufactures power cables, wires, and conductors [S1]. For the quarter ended June 30, 2026 (Q1 FY27), the company reported revenue from operations of ₹1,248 crore [S1] (a news source reported ₹1,428 crore [S5]), a profit after tax of ₹82 crore [S1, S5], and an EBITDA margin of 11.2% [S1, S5].

### Bull case
*   Revenue from operations increased by 18.0% year-on-year (YoY) in Q1 FY27 [S1].
*   The order book grew to ₹3,900 crore as of June 30, 2026, up from ₹3,150 crore a year prior [S1].
*   Exports contributed 21% of revenue in Q1 FY27, an increase from 16% in Q1 FY26 [S1].
*   A new plant in Bharuch, Gujarat, expected to add about 30% to cable capacity, is on track to begin commercial production in Q3 FY27 and will contribute from the second half of FY27 [S1, S2].
*   Management maintains a full-year FY27 revenue growth guidance of 15-17% [S2].
*   Demand from power transmission and data-centre projects remains strong [S1]. Data-centre cables currently represent 9% of domestic revenue and are projected to double in two years [S2].
*   The company's net debt to equity ratio is 0.35 times [S2].
*   Promoter pledged holding has significantly decreased to 4.1% of promoter holding as of June 2026, down from 11.5% in June 2025 [S4].
*   Foreign and Domestic Institutional Investor holdings have increased as of June 2026 [S4].

### Bear case
*   EBITDA margin contracted by 170 basis points to 11.2% in Q1 FY27, primarily due to a sharp rise in copper prices [S1, S2, S5].
*   Receivable days increased from 78 to 96 over the last year, mainly due to slower payments from two large state utility customers [S2].
*   The company received a GST demand order for ₹46.3 crore (including interest and penalty) relating to input tax credit for FY2020-21 and FY2021-22 [S3].
*   Shares of the company fell 3.2% on the NSE after the Q1 FY27 results were announced [S5].

### Open questions
*   There is a material contradiction in reported Q1 FY27 revenue: the company press release states ₹1,248 crore [S1], while a news article reports ₹1,428 crore [S5].
*   While management expects to pass on higher copper costs over the next one to two quarters [S1, S2], the actual impact on future margins is uncertain.
*   The outcome of the appeal against the ₹46.3 crore GST demand order is pending [S3].
*   An older news article from March 2024 reported promoter pledge at 35% [S6], which is significantly higher than the 4.1% reported in the latest shareholding pattern as of June 2026 [S4]. The reason for this discrepancy or the rapid reduction is not explained.
